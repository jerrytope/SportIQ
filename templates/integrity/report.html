What happens next

  1. You get a reference and an access code to check progress, even if anonymous.
  2. A trained safeguarding officer assesses it, usually within 2 working days (urgent child cases sooner).
  3. Where required, concerns are referred to the police, NADC (doping) or the relevant authority.
